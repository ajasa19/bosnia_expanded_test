# Bosnia Expanded Mod

**A Hearts of Iron IV mod dedicated to expanding Bosnia and Herzegovina into a more playable nation.**

Bosnia Expanded aims to build off of Lavender's old Bosnia Expanded mod and give Bosnia and Herzegovina a greater presence in Hearts of Iron IV, providing a more developed experience through expanded national content, events, historical elements, graphics, and gameplay.

---

## Features

The mod's development includes several areas:

- **National Content** – Expanded content for Bosnia and Herzegovina.
- **Events** – Additional events to make gameplay more dynamic.
- **History** – Expanded historical setup and country information.
- **Graphics** – Custom graphical assets.
- **Interface** – Interface elements supporting the mod's content.
- **Localization** – Custom text and localization.
- **Sound** – Additional sound-related content.

More content and improvements are planned as development continues.

## Development

Bosnia Expanded is an ongoing project built/maintained by a single dev on their own time.

The intention is to gradually develop Bosnia into a nation with varying tree paths, events, decisions, etc all while limiting bugs.

Features and content may change as development progresses.

## Installation

### GitHub Installation

1. Download or clone this repository.
2. Extract the mod files.
3. Place the mod folder inside your Hearts of Iron IV mod directory (Documents/Paradox Interactive/Hearts of Iron IV/mod/).
4. Launch the Paradox launcher and add the mod to your playlist.
5. Launch Hearts of Iron IV.

### Steam Installation

1. Go to the HOI4 Workshop
2. Download workshop item (link: TBD)
3. Launch the Paradox launcher and add the mod to your playlist.
5. Launch Hearts of Iron IV.
